# Croissants

- 500 g bread flour
- 280 g butter, cold, beaten into a 15 cm square
- 10 g salt, 55 g sugar, 10 g yeast
- 300 ml cold milk

Lock the cold butter into the dough, then give it
three letter folds, resting 30 minutes in the
fridge between folds. Shape, proof for 2 hours
until wobbly, bake at 200 C for 18 minutes.
